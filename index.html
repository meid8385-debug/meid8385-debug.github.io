Case studies, Saudi market

E-commerce stores on Salla, managed at Gravity Agency. Figures come from the Snapchat Ads Manager, Meta Ads Manager and Salla dashboards.

Snapchat AdsSalla store

Muqtanyati

I took over this account after three months of weaker results. The comparison below is three months before I started (1 Aug to 31 Oct 2025) against five months with me (1 Nov 2025 to 31 Mar 2026).

Snapchat ad spend went up and ROAS went up with it, from 2.47 to 3.94. Salla total sales reached 2.05M, up 1041% on the earlier period, and net profit reached 1.30M.

Per month, ad spend averaged about $16.9K against $3.7K before, and Snapchat purchase value averaged about $66.6K against $9.1K.
